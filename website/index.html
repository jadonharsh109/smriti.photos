That drive in your drawer

Years of photos and videos live on external SSDs and hard drives, in folders nobody opens because there is no way through them. Smriti reads them where they sit and builds the library on top.

  • Nothing is moved. Your folders stay as you arranged them; files are only ever read.
  • Drives are known by identity, not by letter or mount point — unplug and replug and it just works.
  • Browse with the drive unplugged. Thumbnails live on your computer, so the timeline still opens. Plug back in for the originals.
  • Nothing goes missing while a drive is offline. It waits.

Three steps

  • Install it. Python, ffmpeg and everything else is already inside.
  • Plug in your drive and point Smriti at the folder.
  • Let it think. It indexes in the background while you use it.

The first folder downloads the face-recognition models — about 280 MB, once. It is the only download Smriti ever makes.
